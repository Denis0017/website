About Us

Merchex International Private Limited is an international trading and export–import company specializing in salt, agricultural commodities, and selected industrial chemicals.

The company focuses on supplying reliable, high-quality products to global buyers while maintaining strict compliance with international trade regulations, quality standards, and logistics best practices.

Our core strength lies in building dependable supply chains by working closely with manufacturers, processors, and logistics partners across India.

While salt and agricultural commodities remain our primary focus, we also facilitate the sourcing and trading of non-restricted industrial and food-grade chemicals widely used across manufacturing, agriculture, food processing, and industrial applications.

The company was founded by three friends from diverse academic and professional backgrounds, united by a shared ambition—to build something meaningful, enduring, and respected. Rather than following conventional career paths, the founders chose to explore opportunities where discipline, integrity, and execution truly matter.

After extensive market exploration, industry research, and mentorship from experienced professionals, the opportunity in salt and agricultural commodity trading became clear. What began as an idea evolved into a structured enterprise driven by purpose and long-term vision.

Today, Merchex International Private Limited is led by its founding partners and strengthened by industry collaborators with over 30 plus years of experience, along with well-established manufacturers and field-level operational teams. This combination of young leadership and seasoned expertise allows us to operate with agility while maintaining operational depth and reliability.

Our Process

  1. Requirement Assessment — Grade, specifications, volume, destination, and timelines.
  2. Supplier Selection — Identification of producers based on capacity and compliance.
  3. Sampling & Testing — Lab analysis and third-party verification.
  4. Contract Finalization — Pricing, Incoterms, payment terms, and delivery schedules.
  5. Logistics Execution — Packing, transport, customs clearance, and shipment booking.
  6. Delivery & Closure — Shipment tracking, delivery confirmation, and feedback.

Each stage is documented to ensure traceability, accountability, and dispute-free execution.

Compliance & Responsible Trade

Merchex International Private Limited follows strict regulatory practices for all chemical and commodity trading activities.

We ensure compliance with:

  • DGFT Import–Export Regulations
  • DGFT Import–Export Regulations
  • Hazardous Chemical Rules (where applicable)
  • BIS Quality Control Orders
  • International Shipping and Safety Standards
  • Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) Documentation
  • Customs Act & International Trade Documentation Compliance

Our objective is to ensure safe, transparent, and legally compliant global trade operations.
